Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 5th April 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Shia MatchMakr mobile application (the “App”), which allows professional matchmakers to manage candidate profiles for matchmaking purposes. This initiative is community-run and not legally registered as a company.

By using the App, you agree to the collection, use, and sharing of candidate information as described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following types of information entered by matchmakers:

  • Basic candidate information: name, age, contact details
  • Profile photos
  • Dating preferences
  • Private notes or assessments by matchmakers
  • Height, profession, and education

Candidates may also submit their details via a submission form provided by matchmakers.

The App does not automatically collect technical, device, or usage data.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Manage candidate profiles within the App
  • Share candidate profiles with potential matches via one-time image links (e.g., WhatsApp) to preserve privacy
  • Enable communication and notifications for matchmakers
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the App

We do not use data for marketing, analytics, or advertising purposes.

3. Sharing of Information

  • Candidate profiles may be shared only with potential matches via one-time view images to protect privacy.
  • Data is not sold or otherwise shared with third parties.
  • No other matchmakers or external parties have access to candidate information unless explicitly shared by the matchmaker.

4. Data Retention

  • Candidate data is stored only as long as needed to facilitate matchmaking.
  • Matchmakers can request deletion of candidate profiles at any time.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are taken to protect candidate data. Despite precautions, no method of data transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information about minors.
